# Starter catalog content review

## Scope

LP-04 adds three original, project-authored starter sets with 50 cards each:

1. Vietnamese → English fruit vocabulary.
2. Vietnamese → English animal vocabulary.
3. Vietnamese science and social-knowledge questions and answers.

## Review record

- Content version: 1.
- Agent editorial review: complete on 2026-08-19.
- Owner/human language and fact review: pending before enabling `CAPYSTUDY_CATALOG_ENABLED` in production.
- Vocabulary uses singular/canonical English terms and does not include pronunciation, examples or copyrighted course material.
- Science/social facts were limited to stable introductory facts. Current politics, changing statistics, medical/legal advice and disputed claims were excluded.
- Automated checks enforce exact card counts, deterministic positions, no blank sides, no normalized duplicates and no HTML-like content.
- The material was written specifically for CapyStudy and is not copied from a commercial flashcard set.

## Future revisions

Published version 1 is immutable for existing clones. Corrections must publish a new catalog version; they must not overwrite flashcard sets already installed by users.
